How We Build Our Email Community

Perfect Games will grow its list through permission-based sign-ups that offer customers a clear reason to subscribe.

01

Families & Parents

Where/how: Invite parents to sign up during birthday-party inquiries and checkout. Place QR-code signs at the venue and promote the sign-up through family-focused social posts.

Immediate value: A 10% discount on their next party booking.

Long-term value: Birthday inspiration, family promotions, seasonal events, and early notice of special offers.

02

College Students

Where/how: Share a sign-up QR code through campus flyers, student organization partnerships, and Perfect Games social media posts aimed at local students.

Immediate value: A digital coupon for a future visit or student deal.

Long-term value: Student specials, group-night offers, tournament announcements, and affordable entertainment ideas.

03

Businesses & Organizations

Where/how: Add an optional email opt-in to the group-event inquiry form and promote event information through business outreach and LinkedIn.

Immediate value: A group-event planning guide and information about current booking offers.

Long-term value: Corporate event packages, team-building ideas, seasonal booking reminders, and group promotions.
